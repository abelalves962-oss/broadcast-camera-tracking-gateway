# Third-Party Components

This portfolio source references or depends on third-party components that are not automatically redistributed in this repository:

- STMicroelectronics STM32Cube HAL and CMSIS components for STM32 firmware development.
- WIZnet ioLibrary and W5500 Ethernet controller support.
- External middleware used by the embedded build, where applicable.
- .NET and NuGet packages used by the diagnostic monitor.
- The Free-D tracking protocol and related specifications, where applicable.

These components remain subject to the respective licenses, notices, specifications, and terms of their suppliers or authors. Inclusion of a dependency reference here is not a legal determination about redistribution rights. Obtain each component from its official source and review the applicable terms before building or redistributing the project.

Existing third-party notices and license headers in source files must be preserved.
